8-Ball rules
Break the rack. After the first legal group pocket, you are assigned solids or stripes. Clear your group, then legally pocket the 8 to win. Pocketing the cue ball is a scratch and gives the opponent ball-in-hand. Pocketing the 8 early loses the game. A turn continues when you legally pocket your own ball; otherwise it passes.
